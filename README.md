I'm a data scientist and ML engineer building AI for healthcare. By day, I build production ML and LLM systems for federal health agencies, which means most of my commits land in private repos. The pinned projects below are personal work I'm able to share.

## What I Do

- **LLM and NLP Systems**: I build retrieval augmented generation (RAG) pipelines and NLP models over clinical text, EHR, and claims data, and take them from prototype to production.
- **AI/ML Engineering**: I develop and optimize deep learning models, especially in computer vision, from novel CNN architectures for sensor based healthcare monitoring to transfer learning for mobile image recognition.
- **End to End Data Solutions**: From PostgreSQL databases and real time dashboards to cloud data pipelines on AWS and Azure, I turn raw data into insights people can act on.
- **Product & Team Leadership**: I've led technical teams, managed intern cohorts, and contributed to patents for digital health features. I pair technical depth with product vision and clear communication.
- **Applied Research**: I like closing the gap between research and deployment, whether that's generative AI for patient care plans, cancer genomics, or sensor driven algorithms for medical devices.
